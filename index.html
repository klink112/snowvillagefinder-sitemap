Snow Village Finder Guide

This guide publishes free printable Department 56 checklists and a Snow Village value FAQ. Original Snow Village checklists cover pieces introduced from 1976 through the 2020s, including a separate 1985 sheet. Dickens' Village, the North Pole Series, and Christmas in the City each have one full checklist. Village Accessories and Snowbabies are split by decade, and Snow Village Halloween has its own list. Counts and years come from the Snow Village Finder catalog. The 2026 retirements page names all 64 Village pieces retired for 2026, with the same list and photos on Snow Village Finder.
