How much is my Department 56 Snow Village worth?

As of November 2026, common Department 56 Original Snow Village buildings typically ask $48–$127 on eBay, with a $75 median ask. Those are current asking prices, not sold prices; rare early pieces from 1976–1985 can ask much more, up to about $945 for Mission Church.

What is my Snow Village worth?

As of November 2026, common Original Snow Village lighted buildings typically ask $48–$127 on eBay ($75 median). By year introduced: 1976–1985 $32–$67 ($40 median); 1986–1999 $40–$65 ($50 median); 2000–2026 $85–$175 ($116 median). Asking, not sold; condition, box and working lights change value.
