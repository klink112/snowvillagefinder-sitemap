Village Accessories Checklist: 1980s

Pieces introduced 1980–1989 · 16 pieces · Item numbers and years are from the Snow Village Finder catalog.

This printable checklist covers Department 56 Village Accessories introduced in the 1980s. The page lists 16 pieces. Introduction years run from 1986 through 1989: 2 pieces in 1986, 6 in 1987, 3 in 1988, and 5 in 1989. Retirement years run from 1989 through 1997. Fifteen names link to one Snow Village Finder piece page. Village Birds Set of 6 (item 51802) stays unlinked because that item number matches more than one catalog record. The Village Accessories line on Snow Village Finder is the hub for the whole accessory catalog.
